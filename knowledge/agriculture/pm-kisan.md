# PM-KISAN

PM-KISAN provides direct income support of ₹6,000 per year to eligible small and marginal farmer families. The scheme helps stabilize farm incomes and supports rural livelihoods.

## Eligibility

The scheme is available to small and marginal farmer families who own cultivable land. The financial benefit is transferred directly to the bank accounts of eligible farmers in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 every four months.

## How to Apply

Farmers can apply through the PM-KISAN portal, Common Service Centres, or their local agriculture department. The application requires proof of land ownership and bank account details.

## Benefits

Direct income support helps farmers meet input costs such as seeds, fertilizer, and equipment. The scheme also encourages the use of better farming practices and supports the government's larger goal of doubling farmer income.
